How to Latch On

🕷️ Move: touch and hold anywhere — a joystick appears under your finger. Push it to crawl, let go to stop.

🩸 Attack: automatic. Your tick fires sharp keratin spikes at the nearest bug in range — no aiming needed.

🛡️ Carapace Shield: boss abilities are telegraphed — tap the shield button to raise it for a few seconds, blocking all boss-ability damage. It then needs time to recharge, so use it wisely.

💠 Blood orbs: drop from kills. Collect them to level up mid-run and to bank permanent currency.

⬆️ Level up: pick one of three mutations. Your tick also evolves through life stages as it levels.

💀 Permadeath: die and the run ends — but 20% of remaining Blood and all Slime are banked; after beating the final boss, 100% of Blood is kept.

🐕 Dog Shop: spend banked Blood on permanent upgrades between runs.
